More about email design

How email travels
When you send an email, a mail server transfers it toward the recipient's mail server. The recipient can then read it in an email app or browser.
Why subject lines matter
The subject line gives readers a quick idea of what the message contains before they open it.

Parts of an email

To
The “To” field identifies the main recipient of an email. This is where you write the address in the middle of a physical letter.
Cc
“Cc” sends a visible copy to other recipients. Which is useful to other that need to be inform as well without creating other emails.
Bcc
“Bcc” sends a copy without showing those recipients to the others.
